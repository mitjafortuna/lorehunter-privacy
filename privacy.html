Privacy Policy for Lorehunter

Effective date: 15 November 2026

The short version

Lorehunter keeps your game on your phone. It has no accounts, and it never learns who you are. If you leave it switched on, it sends a few anonymous play statistics so the developer can tell whether the game is working, and you can switch them off in Settings at any time. If you choose to watch an ad, the ad comes from Google AdMob, which collects its own information as described below.

What is stored on your phone

The game saves one file on your device: the creatures you have found, your explorers, supplies and upgrades, your settings, and your name if you sign the journal as a patron. It is not backed up to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous play statistics

To learn whether players come back and how far they get, the game sends small batches of statistics when you open it. Each batch contains only:

  • a random number made by the game when it was first opened, so the days of one install can be told apart. It is not taken from your phone, your account or any advertising identifier, and a new one is made if you reinstall,
  • the game's version and whether it runs on Android or iPhone,
  • for each event, what happened (the game was opened for the first time, you came back to it, a creature was found and which number it was, the Patron's Edition was bought, an ad was watched) and how many days after installing it happened.

Nothing else is sent: no name, no email, no location, no device model, no time of day. The server that receives them, run on Cloudflare, stores those fields and the date they arrived. It does not store your IP address or keep request logs. The figures are only ever looked at added up across all players.

Statistics that cannot be sent are kept on the phone and tried again later, and dropped after 30 days.

You can switch statistics off in Settings → Share anonymous play statistics. From that moment nothing more is sent, and anything still waiting on your phone is deleted.

Notifications

If you agree when the game asks, it sets one reminder on your phone: "Your explorers are back", for when your last explorer returns while the game is closed. It is scheduled by your phone itself; no server is involved. You can switch it off in Settings, or in your phone's settings.

Rewarded ads

Free players can choose to watch an ad to double the clues from the next return. Ads are never shown unless you tap for one. They come from Google AdMob.

Nothing is loaded until your first session is over. Then, where the law requires it (the EU, the UK and Switzerland), Google first asks for your consent, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, Google shows only non-personalised ads.

To serve ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, AdMob collects information from your phone, such as its advertising identifier on Android, its IP address (and the approximate location it suggests), and how ads were shown and used. On iPhone the game does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier is not used. How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's own privacy policy covers it.

Patrons never load the ad software at all.

The Patron's Edition

The Patron's Edition is bought through Google Play or the App Store. Google or Apple handle the payment and their own privacy policies cover it. The game only learns that this phone's account owns the Patron's Edition, so it can unlock it and restore it later. It never sees your payment details.

What is not collected

By the game itself: your name, email address or phone number. Your location. Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera. An advertising identifier. A device fingerprint. Crash reports. Cookies. (What AdMob collects when you watch an ad is described under Rewarded ads.)

Children

Lorehunter is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version changes what the game collects, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change with it.
